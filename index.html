About SPOT. SPOT is an education and decision-support tool for healthcare professionals. It supports, and does not replace, clinical judgement, current guidelines or specialist advice. The calculators are risk and screening tools, not diagnostic tests, and must not be used on their own to make treatment decisions. Drug therapy is described at class level as summarised from the cited international guidelines; check the full guideline and local prescribing information before use. Verify all inputs and results before use in patient care.
